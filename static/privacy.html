🔒 Privacy — what happens to your photo

You are about to upload your face to a website run by a classmate. That deserves a straight answer about what happens to it.

The short version

Your photo is used for one thing, for a few seconds, and then thrown away. It is not saved. It is not shown to anyone. It is not sent anywhere else.

Exactly what happens, in order

#WhatKept?
1You choose a photo on your deviceon your device only
2It is sent to the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connectionin transit
3The server finds your face and turns it into 512 numbersin memory
4The server compares those numbers with the archivein memory
5The photo itself is discarded immediatelyno
6You get a list of file names—

What is definitely not done

  • Your photo is not written to disk anywhere — not by this site.
  • Your photo is not stored in a database.
  • Your photo is not shown to anyone, including other users.
  • Your photo is not sent to any third party. The face comparison runs on the same server.
  • There are no advertising trackers and no analytics scripts.
  • You do not need an account, and you are never asked for a name or email.

What is recorded, and why

So that the site can report how many people used it, one line is saved each time someone searches:

  • the date and time
  • a scrambled version of the visitor's network address (a one-way hash, which cannot be turned back into the address)
  • whether the search found anything

The secret used to scramble addresses changes every day. That means the same person on Monday and Tuesday produces two completely unrelated codes, so nobody can be followed from one day to the next. This is not a promise, it is how the code is written.

The numbers are public at Stats. They contain no personal information.

About the archive photos

The graduation photos themselves are not stored on this website. The site only knows which face belongs to which file name. That was a deliberate choice: those photos contain thousands of people who did not choose to be published.

Your rights, practically

If you are in the graduation photos and would rather not be found, or want a specific face removed from the search index, email shivaprasadchannappanavar@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Since nothing identifying is stored, there is not much else to delete — the practical way to "delete my data" is simply to close the page.
