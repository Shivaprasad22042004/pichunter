📁 Bring your own archive

The same tool works for any pile of photographs that people need to search through: another college's convocation, a wedding with 4,000 pictures, a school reunion, a family collection, an event company's backlog.

What it is good at Archives of roughly 500 to 50,000 photos with lots of people in them, where each person only appears in a handful.

Option 1 — I will do it for you, free

If you have an archive that is hard to search, email me. I will index it and host the search page for you at no cost.

What I need from you (the email is pre-filled with these questions):

  • roughly how many photos
  • what the event was, and roughly when
  • how the photos are shared at the moment (Drive folder, WeTransfer, external hard disk, etc.)
  • whether the people in them would be happy to be searchable
  • whether you need it finished by a particular date

What you should know before asking — these are real constraints, not disclaimers:

  • It is hosted on a free server, so each search takes about 15 seconds and the first visit after a quiet period takes about a minute.
  • Indexing takes roughly 1 hour per 5,000 photos.
  • Only file names are returned to people, not the photos themselves. If you want the photos delivered, that needs different hosting and we should discuss it.
  • Faces smaller than about 20 pixels in the original photo cannot be matched. Wide crowd shots from a balcony will mostly not work.
  • I am one person doing this in my spare time. I may say no, or be slow.

Option 2 — Run it yourself

Everything here is open source. If you are comfortable with Docker you can run the whole thing in about twenty minutes.

StepWhat to do
1Clone the repository from Credits
2Put your photos in a folder and run the indexing script (this is the slow part, about 1 hour per 5,000 photos)
3docker build -t photo-finder .
4docker run -p 8000:8000 photo-finder

What you need:

  • About 500 MB of RAM to run the search
  • Python 3.12 for the indexing step
  • Enough disk for the face index — roughly 500 bytes per face (82,620 faces came to 42 MB)

Frequently asked

Can it find people by their clothes?
No. It only looks at faces. A photo of someone in a distinctive dress is not findable by the dress.

Can it handle photos from a phone, in any orientation?
Yes, photos are rotated correctly before the face is read.

How accurate is it?
Very good for clear, front-facing faces, and it is careful not to invent matches. It is not perfect: it will miss photos where the face is turned away, heavily shadowed, or too small to read. On the test archive, a person known to be in a set of ceremony photos was found in all of them, but that is one person, not a scientific measurement.

Will it work on videos?
Not directly. Extract frames first, then index those.

Is it safe for photos of children or a private event?
The technical part is the same, but you should think carefully about whether a searchable index of faces should exist at all. It is easy to restrict access or skip small-face matches. Ask me and we can make it friends-only.
