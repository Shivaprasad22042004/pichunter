📜 About — Graduation Photo Finder

The problem, in one sentence

Somebody took about ten thousand photos at your graduation, and you are in maybe fifteen of them.

Finding those fifteen means scrolling through ten thousand pictures. That takes hours. Most people just give up and never see their own photos.

What this does

You give it one photo of your face. It looks through all ten thousand pictures and tells you the file names of the ones you are in.

It takes about fifteen seconds instead of several hours.

Who made this

A fellow graduate from the same batch, after graduation. It is not an official service and it is not run by the college. Nobody is being charged and nobody is collecting your data.

What it does not do

  • It does not show you the photos. It tells you the file name so you can find them in the shared folder. (Your friends' photos are not hosted anywhere public — that is deliberate.)
  • It does not keep your photo. See Privacy.
  • It does not work for people who were not at the ceremony.
  • It is not perfect. Faces at odd angles, very small faces in crowd shots, or heavy shadow will be missed. That is a limit of the technology, not something that can be fixed by trying harder.

Got your own photo archive?

If your college, department, club or family has a big pile of photos that nobody can search through, the same thing can be built for it. Read Bring your own archive.
